ACCOUNT & DATA

Delete your account

You can delete your CipherCraft account here without the Android app.

What will be removed

  • Your sign-in information and encrypted account keys.
  • Every server-side conversation involving your account, including messages sent by either participant.
  • All sign-in sessions, push registrations and account-specific admin records.

Deletion takes effect in the active service immediately after confirmation. It cannot recall messages someone already saved or notifications already delivered. Unlinked lesson progress and offline work on your device stay as they are.

Use your existing username and password. Suspended accounts can also be deleted. Administrator accounts are protected from self-deletion.

Your account has been deleted

Your sign-in sessions and push registrations have been removed. Your server-side conversations and account keys are gone. Signing up again would create a new account, not restore the deleted one.

Security records and retention

Account identifiers are removed from security counters. Network-based abuse-prevention counters may remain: they stop affecting requests after at most one hour, and expired records are pruned when the service next processes a rate-limited request. They contain no passwords or message content and cannot restore a deleted account. Non-identifying record-number counters remain so new accounts and messages cannot reuse old identities.
