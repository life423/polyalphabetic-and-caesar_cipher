Privacy policy

CipherCraft by drewclark.io · Updated November 25, 2026

Tools and lessons

Cipher tools, hash experiments, lessons and the locally trained AI run on your device. Text and files used only in those tools are not uploaded for model training or analysis. Lesson progress and preferences may be saved locally. Copy and Save use your device clipboard and file picker.

Optional Messages accounts

Messages connects to our Microsoft Azure service. We store your username, an authentication verifier, public encryption key, encrypted private-key backup, session records, conversation participants, encrypted message envelopes, timestamps, read state and blocking state. The raw password and unlocked private key are not sent to the service. Message contents are encrypted on the device before transmission; the service stores encrypted contents and routing metadata.

Your account and conversations are kept until deleted. Administrators can suspend accounts, revoke sessions and delete stored messages; administrative actions generate an audit record. Self-deletion removes account-specific audit records. Classical cipher puzzles are educational and should not be used to protect sensitive information.

Optional Android notifications

If you enable notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging, provided by Google, delivers them. Our server stores your push token, a hashed installation identifier and routing/session information. Registrations expire after 30 days unless refreshed. Sign-out or disabling notifications removes the binding. Notification payloads contain routing IDs and a generic new-message alert, not message contents, cipher keys or your password. Pending deliveries expire after one day.

Service operation and providers

Microsoft Azure hosts the website and messaging service; Google provides Android push delivery. They process network and device information necessary to provide those services, including IP addresses. Our service uses hashed network/account counters for abuse prevention. Network counters have an enforcement window of up to one hour and are physically pruned on a later rate-limited request. Hosting and delivery providers may retain operational records under their own policies. We do not include advertising or analytics tracking SDKs, sell personal data, or send your text to a paid language-model service.

Delete your data

Use Messages → Account settings → Delete my account, or the public account-deletion page. You must confirm your username and password. Deletion removes your server account, sessions, notification registrations and all conversations involving your account, including both participants’ messages. It cannot recall copies already saved by another person or notifications already delivered. Unlinked local lesson progress and offline files remain on your device; clear app/site storage to remove local progress. Protected operator accounts cannot self-delete through this flow; contact us about those records.

Contact and changes

For support, privacy questions or data requests, contact drew@drewclark.io. We update this page when our data practices change. The date above identifies the current policy.
